Risk Triage • 80% Safety Margin Rule

The 3-Tier Execution Gateway

Kaven classifies every proposed trade into one of three deterministic risk tiers before any order touches Binance.

TIER 1 • ALL METRICS ≤ 80% OF LIMITS
⚡ Tier 1: Auto-Execute
All covenants (leverage, concentration, slippage) remain strictly within conservative boundaries. The gateway automatically stamps an RFC-8785 cryptographic signature with a 30-second TTL. Order executes immediately on Binance Spot Testnet with zero human latency.
TIER 2 • WARNING BAND (80% – 100%)
⚠️ Tier 2: Operator Sign-Off
Trade approaches allowable ceilings (e.g. 9x on a 10x BTC cap or 85% of concentration limit). Kaven automatically pauses automation and stages the order in the human operator queue. The operator reviews real-time L2 depth and clicks Approve or Discard.
TIER 3 • ANY METRIC > 100% (BREACH)
🛑 Tier 3: Hard Veto
Proposal breaches hard invariants (e.g. 10x leverage on meme coins, >35% concentration, or flash slippage shock). The trade is dropped immediately. Zero cryptographic tokens are issued, making it physically impossible for any Binance API call to occur.
