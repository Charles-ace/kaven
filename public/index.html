Frequently Asked Questions

Operational details & architecture specifications

Does Kaven hold custody of my funds?

Zero custody. Kaven does not hold private keys or funds. It is an algorithmic pre-flight filter that audits trade proposals before execution.

How does Kaven get market depth?

Kaven connects to Binance Spot and Futures APIs in real time to fetch live order book depth up to 100 levels deep for every proposed symbol.

What happens if a trade is vetoed?

The engine returns a structured VETO response containing exact remediation advice (e.g. "Reduce order size to <= $7,000" or "Reduce leverage on SOLUSDT to <= 3x").
